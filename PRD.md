# PRD — Ankit Nandwani Personal Professional Portfolio & AI Recruiter Assistant

## 1. Product Overview

### Product name

**Ankit Nandwani — Professional Engineering Portfolio**

### Domain

**ankitnandwani.com**

### Product type

A premium, interactive personal professional website designed primarily for:

- Recruiters
- Hiring managers
- Engineering leaders
- Technical interviewers
- Potential collaborators
- Professional networking contacts

The website should function as both:

1. A highly polished professional portfolio.
2. An interactive representation of Ankit's professional experience powered by an AI assistant.

The website must communicate the following positioning:

> **Senior SDET / Automation Engineer with a broad engineering mindset across quality engineering, backend systems, automation, CI/CD, cloud, AI and technology-driven finance projects.**

The site should deliberately avoid looking like a conventional resume website.

It should feel like a **premium engineering product**.

The target emotional reaction is:

> “This person clearly knows engineering, understands modern technology, cares about quality, and has built something unusually polished.”

---

# 2. Product Vision

Build a website that turns a professional resume into an interactive experience.

Instead of simply saying:

> “I have experience with Java, Selenium, API automation and CI/CD.”

the website should allow a recruiter to discover:

- What Ankit has built.
- What problems he has solved.
- How he thinks.
- What technologies he works with.
- How his career evolved.
- How his experience transfers between domains.
- Why he can adapt to unfamiliar technologies.
- What his engineering philosophy is.
- What personal engineering projects he has built.
- Why his finance/trading projects make him relevant to financial-services organizations.
- And finally, ask an AI assistant questions about his professional background.

The website itself should demonstrate engineering ability.

---

# 3. Core Product Principles

## 3.1 Beautiful + useful

The site must never sacrifice usability for visual effects.

Visual design should enhance:

- comprehension
- hierarchy
- discovery
- storytelling
- credibility

not obscure them.

This directly follows CRED's stated philosophy of balancing beauty and function.

---

## 3.2 Form is function

The visual presentation itself should communicate engineering quality.

Examples:

- A career timeline should feel engineered.
- Project cards should expose architecture and technology.
- Metrics should communicate impact.
- Animations should communicate transitions and relationships.
- The AI assistant should feel like a natural part of the product.

---

## 3.3 Curious and informed

The site should encourage exploration.

Visitors should discover progressively deeper information rather than seeing everything at once.

Example:

```text
Hero
 ↓
Career snapshot
 ↓
Major achievements
 ↓
Technical depth
 ↓
Projects
 ↓
Engineering philosophy
 ↓
AI assistant
```

---

## 3.4 Expressive but honest

The design may be bold.

The content must remain factual.

Never invent:

- technologies
- responsibilities
- achievements
- certifications
- employers
- years of experience
- project outcomes
- leadership responsibilities
- production experience

The AI may intelligently explain transferable skills, but it must not manufacture experience.

---

## 3.5 Exciting

The site should create curiosity.

Examples:

> “What exactly does Ankit build?”

> “What happens if I ask his AI assistant about a Staff SDET role?”

> “What is this engineering lab?”

> “How does his trading experience translate to finance?”

The visitor should always have a natural next thing to explore.

# 4. Primary Goals

## Goal 1 — Recruiter conversion

A recruiter should understand within approximately 15–30 seconds:

- Who Ankit is.
- What he specializes in.
- His seniority.
- His strongest technical areas.
- How to contact him.

---

## Goal 2 — Demonstrate engineering depth

The site must demonstrate knowledge beyond a list of skills.

---

## Goal 3 — Demonstrate breadth

Position Ankit as:

**SDET + Automation + Backend + Cloud + CI/CD + AI + Engineering Projects**

rather than only QA/testing.

---

## Goal 4 — Demonstrate adaptability

The website should emphasize the ability to rapidly learn new technologies.

This is particularly important for job-description matching.

---

## Goal 5 — Showcase finance-domain relevance

Trading and finance-related personal projects should be included.

The site should communicate:

> “I don't only understand software engineering; I also understand technology from a market/financial-system perspective.”

However, personal trading activity must not be presented as professional financial-services employment unless explicitly true.

---

## Goal 6 — Create a memorable portfolio

The website should be distinctive enough that a recruiter remembers it after visiting many conventional LinkedIn/resume sites.

# 31. Critical Job-Matching Rule

The system should **never automatically reject Ankit because a job description has gaps**.

However, it must also never fabricate experience.

Instead of:

> “Ankit does not have Kubernetes experience, therefore he is not a match.”

use:

> “The role has a strong overlap with Ankit's experience in automation, CI/CD and cloud engineering. While Kubernetes is not currently listed as a core technology in his profile, his experience across cloud infrastructure and rapidly evolving automation stacks suggests strong transferability. This would be a useful area to explore during the interview.”

The desired strategy is:

**Find the strongest truthful connection.**

Never:

**Invent a connection.**

Never say:

> “Ankit has 5 years of Kubernetes experience”

unless that is actually documented.

# 50. Gemini Recommendation

Gemini is a good fit because:

- low expected traffic
- strong general reasoning
- strong long-context capabilities
- good developer ecosystem
- suitable cost profile
- Google AI Studio/API access

Do not assume a Gemini consumer subscription automatically provides production API credits.

The Gemini API has separate API billing/rate-limit rules and a free tier for selected models.

Start with the most cost-efficient current Gemini model that provides sufficient quality, configured through:

```text
GEMINI_MODEL
```

rather than hard-coding a model name.

# 51. Vector Search

Reasons:

- one fewer external service
- free tier

- sufficient performance
